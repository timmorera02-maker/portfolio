ZorgDichtbij

  • Exploration
  • Web app

A small web app I built next to the mobile medical unit. The cargo bike brings a GP to homeless people; this is how they can reach it.

Someone can ask for medical help, call the unit to where they are, or find a walk-in location nearby. They can also ask for clothes, food or hygiene products, so the team brings what is needed on the same round. No contact details required.

A chatbot answers questions about care and locations, and the team handles the requests in a worker portal.

Lounge bench

  • Exploration
  • Woodwork

A garden bench I designed and built myself at home. Two open side frames carry a seat of 180 cm, and slats close the back and the sides.

I kept it to one board size, 15 by 4 cm, so the whole bench comes out of a short cut list: 62 and 80 cm pieces for the frames, long boards for the seat. Sketch the parts, cut everything to length, build the frames, join, add the slats, stain.

Epoxy serving tray

  • Exploration
  • Material test

A serving tray made from three pieces of burl wood, cast in green epoxy.

The wood goes into a mould, the pigmented resin is weighed, mixed and poured into the gaps, and after curing the block is flattened and sanded smooth. Where the resin runs thin over the wood it turns translucent, which you can see on the back.
